## Visual Style Analysis

### Art Style

* Cinematic anime illustration
* Semi-realistic painterly digital art
* Inspired by Makoto Shinkai, lofi illustrations, and modern editorial posters
* Hand-painted brush texture
* Soft linework with simplified facial features
* Minimal but expressive details

### Color Palette

* Muted teal
* Deep blue
* Cyan
* Olive green
* Warm orange highlights
* Beige skin tones
* Dark navy shadows
* Low saturation
* Strong color harmony

### Lighting

* Golden hour
* Blue hour
* Sunset
* Overcast sky
* Large soft shadows
* Cinematic directional lighting
* Ambient bounce light
* Rim lighting around characters

### Composition

* Vertical (9:16)
* Subject positioned off-center
* Large negative space
* Strong foreground/background separation
* Story-first composition
* One main emotional subject
* Wide environmental view

### Environment

* Empty streets
* Train stations
* Rooftops
* Bridges
* Mountains
* Rainy windows
* Ocean
* Quiet city
* Countryside
* Night city lights

### Mood

* Lonely
* Nostalgic
* Calm
* Reflective
* Emotional
* Hopeful
* Bittersweet
* Peaceful silence

### Characters

* Usually one person
* Sometimes two
* Looking away from camera
* Walking
* Sitting
* Standing alone
* Natural poses
* No exaggerated anime expressions

### Rendering

* Painterly
* Matte finish
* Soft gradients
* Film grain
* Atmospheric haze
* Visible brush strokes
* Rich environmental depth
