# Monetisation Strategy

Don't rely on a single income source.

### 1. Facebook Content Monetisation

Once your page becomes eligible, you may be invited to Facebook's monetisation programmes, which can include ads on eligible content, depending on your country and account status.

### 2. Brand Sponsorships

Promote:

* Learning apps
* Financial services
* Books
* Mental wellness apps
* Productivity tools

### 3. Affiliate Marketing

Recommend books, courses, or software and earn commissions when people buy through your links.

### 4. Sell Digital Products

Examples:

* Motivation eBooks
* Journals
* Habit trackers
* Goal planners
* Printable affirmation cards

### 5. Online Courses

Teach:

* Public speaking
* Self-improvement
* Productivity
* Video editing
* Social media growth

### 6. Cross-Platform Growth

Repurpose every video to:

* Facebook
* Instagram Reels
* YouTube Shorts
* TikTok (where available)

One video can generate views across multiple platforms.

# Video Editing Style

* Fast cuts every 2–4 seconds
* Bold subtitles
* Emotional background music
* Zoom effects

---

# Content Sources

Avoid copying other creators' videos. Instead:

* Write original scripts.
* Use your own narration or licensed AI voices.
* Use licensed or self-created visuals.
* Use royalty-free music.
* Add your own editing and commentary.

Original content is much more likely to qualify for monetisation than reposted videos.

---

# 90-Day Growth Plan

## Month 1

* Publish 3–5 videos daily.
* Build a consistent visual style.
* Learn from analytics to identify your best-performing topics.

## Month 2

* Increase to 4–6 videos daily if you can maintain quality.
* Start responding to comments and encouraging discussion.
* Repurpose every video to other platforms.

## Month 3

* Focus on your top-performing themes.
* Apply for available monetisation programmes if eligible.
* Begin affiliate marketing and digital product sales.

---

# Long-Term Brand Vision

Rather than just running a Facebook page, build **IAMYOUNZ** into a content brand:

* **Facebook:** Long-form videos and community
* **Instagram:** Reels and quotes
* **YouTube:** Shorts and longer motivational videos
* **Website:** Blog, email newsletter, and digital products
* **Email List:** Weekly motivation and product launches

This approach gives you multiple income streams instead of depending on one platform.

## Estimated Timeline

If you consistently publish original, high-quality content:

* **0–3 months:** Build your first engaged audience.
* **3–6 months:** Reach eligibility for some monetisation opportunities if your content performs well and meets platform requirements.
* **6–12 months:** Grow income through a combination of Facebook monetisation, sponsorships, affiliate marketing, and your own digital products.

The fastest sustainable path is to create original videos consistently, optimise based on performance, and diversify your revenue rather than trying to maximise Facebook payouts alone.
