# Content Formula for Every Video

Use this structure consistently to improve watch time:

1. **Hook (0–3 seconds):** A surprising statement or question.
2. **Problem (3–15 seconds):** Describe a relatable situation.
3. **Story (15–60 seconds):** Share an original anecdote or scenario.
4. **Life Lesson (60–90 seconds):** Explain the key takeaway.
5. **Call to Action (Final 5 seconds):** "Follow **IAMYOUNZ** for daily emotional stories and life lessons."

### Pro Tips

* Use original scripts instead of copying other creators.
* Add captions to every video—many viewers watch with the sound off.
* Keep a consistent visual style (logo, fonts, colours, intro).
* Review your analytics weekly and create more videos around the topics with the highest watch time and shares.
* Repurpose each video for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok (where available) to maximise reach from a single piece of content.
